# Pricing Rules & Analysis Logic

Derived from real Cape Coral / Turtle Creek work. Adapt for your market.

## Core Principles
- Always include your own listing in every calendar pull.
- Prioritize quiet, non-waterfront, 3BR/2BA heated-pool comps sleeping 6-7 in the same SE/central area.
- Weight by Match_Score (capacity penalty for larger homes).
- Drop delisted / zero-rate comps from future pulls; keep historical snapshots.
- Enforce local min stay (Cape Coral: 6 nights).
- Cost control: Mon/Thu pulls, hard $30/month ceiling during trial.

## Gap Analysis
For next 30 / 60 / 90 days (available nights only):
1. Compute weighted average rate of active core comps (exclude self).
2. Compare to own listing average on the same nights.
3. Flag if own rate is >10-15% above weighted core average (near-term friction for new listings).
